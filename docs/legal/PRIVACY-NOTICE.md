# Customer privacy notice (master text) — version 3

> **Draft for checking.** Have it reviewed before real customers use the service. The in-product version is `PrivacyNotice` in `components/assist/consent.tsx`. Keep both in step, and bump `NOTICE_VERSION` when the wording changes; each customer's consent records the version they saw.
>
> `{brand}`, `{evidenceDays}`, `{transcriptDays}`, `{supportLine}` and `{reference}` are filled in per client and per session.

**Who is responsible.** {brand} is responsible for your information (the controller). Absolute Intelligence UK Ltd runs this visual support service for {brand}. It only uses your information on {brand}'s instructions (the processor).

**What we use.**
- Your camera pictures and microphone during the session.
- What you say and type.
- The still pictures needed for each step, and what the assistant read in them.
- The steps taken and the session reference.

We don't record video and we don't use your information for marketing.

**Why.**
- To help with the problem you contacted {brand} about.
- To hand you over to a member of the team when needed.
- To keep a record of the help given.
- To check and improve the quality of the service.

The legal basis is {brand}'s legitimate interest in supporting its customers. Where you are a customer, it is also providing the service under your contract.

**Who else sees it.**
- {brand}'s support team.
- Anthropic: the AI that reads the pictures and words.
- ElevenLabs: the assistant's voice.
- Our hosting, database and text message providers.

Some of them are in the United States. Transfers are protected by the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or the UK–US data bridge. None of them may use your information for their own purposes.

**How long.**
- Still pictures are kept for {evidenceDays} days, then deleted. If {brand} chose "live only", pictures are not kept at all.
- The conversation transcript is kept for {transcriptDays} days, then deleted.
- A short record that the session took place is kept for audit.

**Your choices and rights.** You can say no, use voice only, ask for a person, or end the session at any time.

You can ask {brand} to:
- give you a copy of your information;
- correct it or delete it;
- stop using it (object).

Call {supportLine} and quote your reference {reference}. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
