# Visual Assist: test it in 10 minutes

You play both people in a support call: **the customer** (on your phone) and **the support agent** (on your laptop).

**Address:** https://visual-assist-7viw.onrender.com
**Login:** press **Try it now**, then **Log in**. The email and password are already filled in.

> If the first page is slow to open, give it a minute; after that it is quick.

## 1. Start (laptop)

1. Log in. You land on **Try Visual Assist**.
2. Choose **Real AI with a real camera** (or **Guided example** for a ready-made story).
3. Press **Start the conversation**.
4. Press **Turn on alerts** at the top, so the laptop chimes when the customer asks for a person.
5. Press **Open agent screen** and leave that tab open.

The blue box at the top always says **whose turn it is and what to do next**.

## 2. Be the customer (phone)

1. On the laptop, press **Copy link for your phone** and open it on your phone (or scan, or text it to yourself).
2. Tap **Agree and continue**, then **Turn on camera** (or **Continue with voice only**).
3. Tap the screen once so the assistant can speak. You should **hear** a British voice.
4. Say or type the problem, for example *"My broadband isn't working."*
5. Follow the instructions. Point the camera at the router; after each picture a line appears: **"I saw: …"**. Check it is right.

**Try these too:**
- Turn the camera off, or say *"I can't show you"*. The assistant asks you to describe the lights instead.
- Show something blurry or far away. A good assistant asks for a better view instead of guessing.

## 3. Ask for a person (phone, then laptop)

1. On the phone, tap **Human help**.
2. The laptop chimes and shows a red **Join now** banner.
3. Press **Join now**, then **Join session**. You are now the agent.
4. The phone shows and says your hello. You can see the customer's camera live, read everything the AI did, and type messages.
5. Press **End**, then **Resolved**.

## 4. Look at the record (laptop)

Open **Sessions** in the portal. Every conversation shows what was said, what the AI saw and how sure it was, and any actions taken.

## What to tell us

For anything odd, send: **what you did**, **what you expected**, **what happened**, and the session reference (for example **VA-12461**, shown at the top of the agent screen). A screenshot helps.

Especially:
- Did you **hear** the assistant? Was it the same voice every time?
- Did the **"I saw"** line match what was really in front of the camera?
- Was it always clear what to do next?
- Did the alert sound and the hand-over to a person work?
