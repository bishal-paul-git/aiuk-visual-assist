# Positioning: Absolute Intelligence Visual Assist

## What it is

A **visual support agent**: the customer shows a problem on their phone camera, an AI guides them through an approved fix step by step, and a person takes over, with full context, when the AI shouldn't or can't finish.

Underneath is a reusable engine: governed workflows, a vocabulary of what the AI may report, reading guides, approved answers, policies, confidence checks, human hand-over, business actions and a full audit record. A new industry is a new **workflow pack**, not a new product.

## Where it belongs

**Category:** visual customer support / remote resolution for physical, in-home problems.
**Buyer:** Head of Customer Operations or Field Service in a UK regulated home-services company.
**Value:** fewer engineer visits and repeat calls, faster first-contact resolution, and a recorded evidence trail.

**One-line positioning:**
> Visual Assist resolves in-home faults on the first contact: the customer shows the problem, approved AI guides the fix, and a person steps in with the full picture when needed. Fewer engineer visits, nothing repeated, everything on record.

## Go to market: one industry first, built as a platform

Sell it as a **vertical solution**, but build it as a **platform**. Buyers trust "built for broadband faults" far more than "an AI agent for anything". Accuracy comes from narrow, well-defined jobs with their own reading guides, approved answers and a measured photo test set.

| Order | Industry | First job | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (now) | **Broadband / telecom** | Router and hub faults | Highest-volume visual support call; every avoided engineer visit is roughly £100+; the prototype is built and tested |
| 2 | **Energy** | Boiler fault codes, smart meter displays | Fits the existing UK utilities client base; boiler workflow already drafted |
| 3 | **Water** | Meter reads, leak and stop-tap checks | Same buyers and procurement as energy |
| 4 | **Insurance** | Claim evidence (first notice of loss) | Different value (evidence and fraud), needs its own policy work |

The same engine supports each one. Each industry gets its own:
- workflow;
- vocabulary and reading guides;
- approved answers;
- policy;
- test set of 30–50 labelled photos, with a published accuracy figure.

## What makes it credible (and what competitors lack)

- The AI never improvises a fix: it follows the client's approved steps and its forbidden list.
- It says "I can't see that clearly" instead of guessing, and shows the customer what it read ("I saw: Internet light: off").
- Hand-over to a person at any time, with the conversation, photos and AI readings already in front of them.
- Every reading, decision and action is recorded, with how sure the AI was.
- It works without the camera too (by description).
